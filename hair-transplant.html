Comprehensive Hair Transplant Guide

An objective medical overview of surgical hair restoration: physiological mechanisms, candidate evaluation, surgical methodologies, and postoperative healing milestones.

Medical Overview

How Hair Restoration Works

Androgenetic alopecia (pattern hair loss) occurs when genetically susceptible scalp hair follicles miniaturize under the influence of the androgen hormone dihydrotestosterone (DHT).

Crucially, follicles located across the occipital and parietal regions (the permanent donor zone at the lower back and sides of the head) lack the cellular receptors that trigger DHT sensitivity.

When these resistant follicular units are meticulously extracted and transplanted to thinning areas, they retain their intrinsic genetic resilience. Once healed and established, they produce permanent, natural hair for a lifetime.

Patient Suitability

Are You a Good Candidate?

Successful hair restoration requires realistic expectations and sufficient biological donor capacity.

1. Donor Area Density

The permanent donor zone must contain adequate follicular density (ideally 70+ follicular units per cm²) to safely supply recipient sites without causing visible thinning in the back.

2. Stability of Hair Loss

Candidates with stabilized hair loss patterns—often supported by medical therapy—achieve more predictable, harmoniously aged long-term aesthetic outcomes.

3. Scalp Health & Elasticity

A healthy scalp free of active inflammatory dermatological conditions (such as lichen planopilaris or active alopecia areata) provides an optimal vascular environment for graft take.

Regrowth Milestones

12-Month Post-Op Recovery Timeline

Understanding the physiological cycle of newly transplanted hair helps alleviate common patient concerns during healing.

Days 1–7

Initial Healing

Tiny micro-crusts form at graft sites. Gentle specialized wash protocol begins on Day 3 to loosen scabs. Most swelling resolves by Day 5.

Weeks 2–8

The "Shock Loss" Phase

The transplanted hair shafts naturally shed as the follicular bulb enters the telogen (resting) phase. This is completely expected and normal.

Months 3–6

Early Regrowth

Fine new hairs begin emerging from the dormant follicles. Hair shafts gradually thicken in caliber and pigmentation month by month.

Months 9–12

Full Density & Maturation

Complete aesthetic density, natural texture blending, and mature hair caliber are fully realized. Final follow-up evaluation with Dr. Syed Imran Zahid.

Medical Safety

Essential Post-Operative Aftercare

The first 10 days post-procedure are critical for graft stabilization. We provide each patient with a specialized medical aftercare kit and 24/7 clinical contact.

  • Sleep elevated at 30°–45° for the first 3 nights
  • Use clinical saline spray every 2 hours to keep grafts hydrated
  • Avoid rubbing, scratching, or picking recipient crusts
  • Refrain from heavy weightlifting or contact sports for 14 days
