Understanding Hair Restoration Techniques

Objective comparisons to help you understand the distinctions between modern surgical modalities.

Feature / Aspect FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) DHI (Direct Hair Implantation)
Extraction Method Individual micro-punch (0.7 - 0.85 mm) Individual micro-punch (0.7 - 0.85 mm)
Implantation Technique Custom sapphire blade incisions followed by graft placement with microsurgical forceps Direct insertion via Choi Implanter Pen with simultaneous incision and graft delivery
Shaving Requirement Usually donor and recipient shaved (unshaven options available) Often donor-only shaved (ideal for planting between existing hairs)
Ideal Candidate Moderate to extensive hair loss areas requiring high graft volume Hairline definition, high-density touch-ups, beard, and eyebrow work
Typical Healing Phase Scabs shed within 7–10 days; redness resolves within 2 weeks Micro-crusts shed in 6–8 days; rapid epidermal recovery

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent medical answers regarding candidacy, procedure day, recovery, and results.

A hair transplant is an outpatient microsurgical procedure that relocates genetically resistant follicular units from donor areas (typically the back and sides of the scalp) to thinning or balding recipient zones. Once transplanted, these follicles retain their genetic resistance to dihydrotestosterone (DHT) and continue to grow naturally.
Both techniques extract individual follicular units. In traditional FUE, recipient channels are created first using ultra-fine micro-blades, and grafts are then manually placed. In DHI, the extracted graft is loaded into a hollow Choi implanter pen that creates the channel and implants the follicle simultaneously. Your doctor will recommend the ideal modality during consultation.
Most procedures take between 5 to 8 hours depending on the graft count required (ranging from 1,500 to 4,000+ grafts). The day is divided into donor extraction, a rest break with lunch provided, and recipient site implantation.
Most patients resume sedentary work or desk duties within 3 to 5 days. Any visible micro-crusting sheds between days 7 and 10. Strenuous exercise, heavy lifting, swimming, and direct sunlight exposure should be avoided for 2 to 3 weeks.
Transplanted hair follicles from the permanent donor zone are genetically resistant to DHT and generally continue growing for a lifetime. However, untreated native hairs surrounding the transplanted area may continue to thin naturally with age, which is why medical maintenance therapy may be recommended.
Treatment costs vary depending on the individual's needs, donor availability, degree of hair loss, and the treatment plan chosen. Contact us for a personalized consultation and transparent quotation.
