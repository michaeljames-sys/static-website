Medical Disclaimer

Important Information Regarding Clinical Guidance and Outcomes

Important Patient Notice

The information contained on this website is intended solely for educational, informational, and general interest purposes. It does not replace individualized clinical judgment, medical diagnosis, or treatment recommendations from a qualified physician.

1. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Viewing the contents of this website, reviewing before-and-after photographs, or submitting an electronic inquiry through our contact forms does not establish a formal doctor-patient relationship with Dr. Syed Imran Zahid or Hair Art. A medical relationship is established only upon formal clinical consultation and mutual agreement in our medical office.

2. Variation in Individual Outcomes

Hair restoration surgery outcomes vary significantly across individual patients. Final follicular yield, coverage density, and aesthetic satisfaction depend on a range of biological variables, including but not limited to:

  • Individual donor area follicular density and hair shaft diameter
  • Underlying scalp health, elasticity, and dermal micro-vascularization
  • Patient age, genetic progression of androgenetic alopecia, and hormone levels
  • Strict adherence to postoperative washing, medication, and activity guidelines

3. No Medical Guarantees

Hair Art does not guarantee specific mathematical graft yields, exact regrowth counts, or permanent cessation of ongoing native hair miniaturization. As with all medical procedures, surgical hair restoration carries standard surgical considerations which will be thoroughly discussed with you prior to any scheduled treatment.

4. Medical Emergencies

This website and its web inquiry forms are not monitored 24/7 for acute medical emergencies. If you are experiencing an acute medical emergency or severe post-surgical complications, call your local emergency services (such as 911 or local emergency number) immediately or proceed to the nearest emergency hospital.
