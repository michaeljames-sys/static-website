Hair Transplant Methods

Specialized Hair Restoration Methods

Physician-led scalp hair restoration in Faisalabad. At Hair Art, we specialize strictly in 4 advanced hair transplant methods to ensure optimal follicular yield, zero trauma, and permanent natural density.

Maximum Grafts

FUT Method

Follicular Unit Transplantation (Strip Surgery) with high-power stereo-microscope dissection. Harvests maximum follicle count in a single session with trichophytic fine-line closure.

View FUT Method Details
Microsurgical FUE

FUE Method

Individual micro-punch follicular unit extraction that leaves no linear scar. Ideal for high graft yields, natural density, and active lifestyles.

View FUE Procedure
Direct Implantation

DHI Method

Direct insertion using specialized Choi implanter pens. Enables maximum control over angle, depth, and direction without prior channel incisions.

View DHI Procedure
Micro-Needle Implanter

Chinese Method

Specialized Chinese micro-needle implantation (0.6mm–0.8mm) delivering 98%+ graft survival, 360° natural angle control, zero root trauma, and rapid 48-hour recovery.

View Chinese Method Guide

Understanding Hair Restoration Techniques

Side-by-side comparison of extraction methods, graft survival, and recovery periods across our four specialized techniques.

Feature / Aspect FUT Method FUE Method DHI Method Chinese Method
Extraction Method Stereo-microscope dissection from narrow donor strip Individual micro-punch (0.7 - 0.85 mm) Individual micro-punch (0.7 - 0.85 mm) Ultra-fine micro-punch (0.6 - 0.8 mm) with zero root trauma
Implantation Technique Microsurgical forceps into pre-made recipient micro-incisions Custom sapphire blade incisions followed by graft placement with forceps Direct insertion via Choi Implanter Pen with simultaneous incision & delivery High-density Chinese micro-needle implanter with precise 360° angle control
Shaving Requirement Donor strip trimmed only (surrounding hair fully conceals it) Usually donor and recipient shaved (unshaven options available) Often donor-only shaved (ideal for planting between existing hairs) Donor-only or completely unshaven options available
