Our Hair Restoration Treatments

Physician-performed surgical procedures and non-invasive trichology therapies tailored to address every stage of hair loss.

Microsurgical FUE

FUE Hair Transplant

Individual micro-punch follicular unit extraction that leaves no linear scar. Ideal for high graft yields, natural density, and active lifestyles.

View FUE Procedure
Direct Implantation

DHI Hair Transplant

Direct insertion using specialized Choi implanter pens. Enables maximum control over angle, depth, and direction without prior channel incisions.

View DHI Procedure
Facial Restoration

Beard & Mustache Transplant

Reconstruct patchy or thin facial hair using single-hair grafts oriented along natural facial grain for sharp, masculine contours.

View Beard Procedure
Delicate Micro-Grafts

Eyebrow Restoration

Ultra-precise single follicular unit placement placed almost parallel to the skin (15° angle) to achieve natural arch symmetry.

View Eyebrow Procedure

Hair Loss Consultation
