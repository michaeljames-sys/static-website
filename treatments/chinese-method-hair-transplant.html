What is the Chinese Method Hair Transplant?

The Chinese Method Hair Transplant is a cutting-edge, minimally invasive follicular restoration technique that utilizes specialized 0.6mm to 0.8mm medical-grade alloy micro-needles. Unlike traditional hair transplant techniques, the Chinese method unites micro-incision creation and follicular implantation into a synchronized, single-step motion.

At Hair Art in Faisalabad, under the direct medical supervision of Dr. Syed Imran Zahid (MBBS, RMP, RPMDC) and Dr. Kashif Hussain (MBBS, FCPS Dermatology with 10 years experience), this technique is performed with high-power surgical loupes to achieve extraordinary graft density (up to 55–65 follicular units per cm²) and undetectable hairline integration.

Traditional Method vs. Chinese Synchronized Implantation

Traditional transplants use a two-step sequence: cutting a hole with a scalpel, then using forceps to grab the hair bulb and push it in—often causing bulb crush, transection, and bent roots. The Chinese Method eliminates forceps handling completely: the follicle is loaded into the hollow micro-needle and implanted directly with zero bulb trauma.

  • 360° Direction Control: Hair follicles can be aligned precisely to mirror natural crown whorls, cowlicks, and temporal peaks.
  • Minimal Out-of-Body Ischemia: Streamlined handling cuts follicle holding time in half, maximizing cellular vitality and long-term yield.

Step-by-Step Surgical Protocol at Hair Art

Every Chinese Method procedure is personally planned and conducted by Dr. Syed Imran Zahid and Dr. Kashif Hussain:

01

High-Magnification Digital Trichoscopy

We map donor safe zones, assess follicular density per cm², and design an age-appropriate hairline aligned with your facial geometry.

02

Buffered Tumescent Local Anesthesia

Proprietary pH-buffered comfort anesthesia delivers a 100% painless experience with zero systemic drowsiness.

03

Atraumatic Micro-Harvest

Individual grafts are harvested using precision micro-punches that leave donor scalp tissue fully intact and unscarred.

04

Hypothermic Electrolyte Graft Preservation

Follicles are preserved in chilled, nutrient-rich holding media that maintains cellular respiration and ATP levels.

05

Single-Step Micro-Needle Implantation

Follicles are loaded into 0.6mm–0.8mm implanters and positioned at the calibrated angle, depth, and direction in a single motion.

06

Clinical Follow-Up & Care Protocol

First clinical hair wash on Day 3, customized aftercare kit, and progress evaluations scheduled at 3, 6, and 12 months.
