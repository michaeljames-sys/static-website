FUE Hair Transplant in [City]

Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE): The gold-standard microsurgical hair restoration method delivering maximum graft viability, minimal downtime, and zero linear scarring.

The Technique

Micro-Precision Follicular Extraction

In modern FUE, each follicular unit containing 1 to 4 hairs is individually harvested using ultra-refined hollow micro-punches ranging from 0.7mm to 0.85mm in diameter.

Unlike historic strip harvesting (FUT), FUE leaves tiny circular micro-wounds that close and re-epithelialize within 48 to 72 hours, resulting in faint pinpoint dots virtually undetectable even with short clipper fades.

Recipient sites are created using high-precision sapphire blades, which produce smaller incisions with cleaner borders, less tissue edema, and smoother healing.

Clinical Advantages

Why Choose FUE at [CLINIC_NAME]?

No Linear Scar

Retain the freedom to wear your hair closely cropped or faded without worrying about a prominent surgical strip scar across the back of the scalp.

Swift Healing & Low Downtime

Donor extraction sites heal rapidly within days. Most patients return to regular social and non-strenuous professional activities within 3 to 5 days.

Selective Graft Harvesting

We selectively harvest single-hair units specifically for delicate hairline softening, and 2-to-3 hair units for dense core coverage.

Procedure Day

What to Expect on Treatment Day

FUE is an outpatient procedure performed under localized numbing. Patients remain awake, comfortable, and can listen to audio or watch movies throughout the day.

  • Morning: Hairline design confirmation and gentle donor graft extraction
  • Midday: Nutritional rest break and microscopic graft sorting
  • Afternoon: Artistic recipient implantation by our physician team
  • Evening: Detailed post-op review, wash kit handover, return home
