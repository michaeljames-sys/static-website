FUT Hair Transplant Method in Faisalabad

Follicular Unit Transplantation (FUT) remains the gold standard for harvesting the maximum number of intact follicular grafts in a single surgical session, ideal for extensive thinning and high-density coverage.

Strip Micro-Surgery

How the FUT Method Works

During the FUT procedure, a narrow, linear strip of donor tissue is harvested from the permanent safe donor zone at the back of the head. Our surgical team utilizes an advanced trichophytic closure technique, allowing native hair to grow directly through the fine scar line, rendering it virtually unnoticeable beneath standard hairstyles.

The harvested donor strip is immediately transferred to a temperature-regulated sterile dissecting station where experienced technicians dissect individual 1, 2, and 3-hair follicular units under high-magnification binocular stereomicroscopes. This microscopic precision minimizes follicular transection rates to below 2%, delivering the highest graft survival yield available in modern hair restoration.

Once isolated, Dr. Syed Imran Zahid and our clinical team meticulously create recipient sites matching your natural hair emergence angles, direction, and density gradients.

Why Choose the FUT Method at Hair Art?

  • ✓ Maximum Graft Yield: Harvest 3,000 to 4,500+ follicular grafts in a single procedure.
  • ✓ Superior Follicle Preservation: Stereo-microscopic dissection ensures near-zero transection.
  • ✓ Preserves Donor Zone: Leaves the surrounding parietal and occipital scalp intact for future procedures if needed.
  • ✓ Cost Efficiency: Delivers superior graft numbers per session, offering unmatched value for extensive baldness (Norwood IV-VII).

FUT Procedure Overview

  • Procedure Type Strip Follicular Micro-Surgery
  • Anesthesia Local Anesthesia (Painless)
  • Session Duration 5 – 7 Hours (Outpatient)
  • Recovery Time 7 – 10 Days (Suture Removal)
  • Final Results 10 – 12 Months (Lifelong Growth)
